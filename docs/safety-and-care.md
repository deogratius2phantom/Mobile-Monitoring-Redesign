# Safety and Care

This repository contains a hardware design for a mobile monitoring device with radio modules and power circuitry. Treat prototypes and assembled boards with the same care you would give any RF-enabled electronics project.

## Handling

- Protect the board from electrostatic discharge during assembly and inspection.
- Avoid flexing the PCB when modules, headers, or antenna connectors are installed.
- Keep conductive debris away from exposed electronics and test fixtures.

## Power precautions

- Verify voltage, polarity, and current capability before first power.
- If your build uses a rechargeable battery, use charging and protection circuitry appropriate to that chemistry.
- Do not leave early prototypes unattended while verifying first power or GSM transmit behavior.

## RF and antenna considerations

- Follow local regulations for wireless devices and SIM-based connectivity.
- Keep antennas clear of large metal obstructions and enclosure features that may reduce performance.
- Avoid unnecessary handling of the antenna path while the device is operating.

## Environmental care

- Protect the device from moisture, contamination, and extreme temperatures unless your enclosure and sensor choices are designed for that environment.
- If the project is deployed outdoors, validate enclosure sealing and venting before field use.

## Storage and transport

- Store boards and modules in anti-static packaging when possible.
- Secure loose antennas, cables, and daughter boards before transport.
- Label test units clearly so unfinished prototypes are not mistaken for fully validated hardware.
